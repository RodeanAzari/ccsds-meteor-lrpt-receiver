The whole receiver on the Arty A7-100T (`arty_rx_eth_top` with the ingest reorder buffer, ADR 0013, and the NCO word set per replay by SET_NCO, ADR 0014) turned a recording replayed over Ethernet into CADUs sent back over Ethernet. The recording is `make synthetic-iq`'s (300 CADUs of OQPSK at 8 dB Eb/N0, 151 kHz with a 30 Hz/s ramp, 34 s; SatDump 1.2.2 decoded 258 of its 300 frames, `docs/log.md` 2026-11-03). In all 15 replays, each from a freshly programmed board, asked at 1×, 5× and 5.6× real time (achieved 1.00×, 3.38–4.18× and 5.14–5.53×: the host was loaded, the column `achieved_speed` has each), the board sent 298 CADU packets with consecutive pkt_seq and every one matched a sent frame on all 892 data bytes, with **no Reed-Solomon correction at all** and no codeword uncorrectable. The first frame is lost to the loops' pull-in and the last is cut by the end of the recording, as in simulation. Every datagram was placed in sequence (ingest_gap, ingest_lost and ingest_late 0; ingest_ok = 68,266 = all sent), no eth_rx overflow, no frame-sync slip or dropped peak. The previous campaign (2026-11-08, ingest without the reorder buffer, same command) needed 9 to 84 corrected symbols in the 8 of 15 replays where the host reordered datagrams; whether the host reordered any this time is not counted, only that none reached the receiver out of order. One replay (5×, row 10) began with 14 MAC restarts: the eth_rx FIFO came up corrupted at link-up (request 0005 to fpga-eth-core) and the stopgap re-reset the MAC before the first replay datagram (the board's first stats packet already shows 14, with one frame received); that replay then decoded like the others. The input is synthetic: this proves the receiver on hardware, not on a real Meteor signal.
