The RRC matched filter RTL (`rtl/sync/sync_rrc.sv`) matches `model/rrc_fixed.py` bit for bit on 16,000 samples: the bit-true DDC output of OQPSK frames at 6 dB Eb/N0 with a 151 kHz carrier and a 30 Hz/s ramp. Random stalls ran on both sides of the stream. It reuses the comp FIR's time-shared multiply-accumulate with the 57 RRC taps, so it takes 62 clocks per 256 kS/s sample: 6.3× real time at 100 MHz. That sets the RRC-input injection mux's ceiling for BER campaigns below the ~22× the 100 Mb/s link could carry at 256 kS/s. At 6.3×, the 10⁸ decoded bits a 10⁻⁶ point needs take about 220 s, so it is not worth optimizing yet. Folding the symmetric taps through the DSP pre-adder would halve it.
