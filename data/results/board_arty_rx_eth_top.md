The whole receiver as a board design (`rtl/top/arty_rx_eth_top.sv`: fpga-eth-core RX/TX, the reorder-buffer ingest of ADR 0013, `ctrl_rx` for SET_NCO commands (ADR 0014), DDC, `rx_chain` with fpga-rs-core, CADU and stats packets, `pkt_arb2`) places and routes on the Arty A7-100T at 100 MHz with timing met: WNS +0.256 ns, WHS +0.025 ns, no failing endpoints and no critical warnings. It uses 13,682 LUTs (21.6% of 63,400), 13,984 flip-flops, 29 DSP48E1 (12.1% of 240), 8 RAMB36 and 7 RAMB18; `ctrl_rx` is 19 LUTs and 117 flip-flops (the build before it, with the NCO word as a build parameter: 13,597 LUTs, WNS +0.224 ns). The worst setup path is new: the ingest's 64-bit `seq` window compare (`seq_q` → `far` → `s_tready`) into `ctrl_rx`'s counter enables, which snoop the same handshake, 9.4 ns with 48% logic. If it ever goes negative, registering `far` (a datagram's seq is complete 1 byte before its first sample byte) removes it. The NCO word is no longer built in: the bitstream starts at 150 kHz and the host sets each recording's word with SET_NCO before the replay, so one build serves every recording.
