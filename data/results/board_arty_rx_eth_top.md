# board_arty_rx_eth_top

- **Commit:** `766147ff9b37bbec56ece16cd9fcb535542a8a44 (with uncommitted changes)`
- **Command:** `make board BOARD_TOP=arty_rx_eth_top BOARD_GENERICS="FREQ_WORD=32'h25C11200"`
- **Tools:** Python 3.14.4, NumPy 2.5.4, SciPy 1.18.1, Vivado 2025.2
- **Input:** synthetic, no input data: board-level place and route (constr/arty_a7_100t.xdc) of arty_rx_eth_top with FREQ_WORD=32'h25C11200 on xc7a100tcsg324-1. No real recording.

The whole receiver as a board design (`rtl/top/arty_rx_eth_top.sv`: fpga-eth-core RX/TX, the reorder-buffer ingest of ADR 0013, DDC, `rx_chain` with fpga-rs-core, CADU and stats packets, `pkt_arb2`) places and routes on the Arty A7-100T at 100 MHz with timing met: WNS +0.224 ns, WHS +0.028 ns, no failing endpoints and no critical warnings. It uses 13,597 LUTs (21.4% of 63,400), 13,669 flip-flops, 29 DSP48E1 (12.1% of 240), 8 RAMB36 and 7 RAMB18; the ingest's 4-slot ring is one RAMB36 (the build before it, with a 2-slot ring in a RAMB18: 13,543 LUTs, WNS +0.580 ns). The worst setup path is not the ingest's: it is the one the rx_chain's own build reports (`synth_rx_chain.csv`), fec_cadu's distributed-RAM CADU buffer read into the RS syndrome accumulators, 8.9 ns of which 82% is routing, so its slack moves with placement. If it ever goes negative, a register on the buffer's read data removes it. This build carries the synthetic recording's NCO word (`FREQ_WORD=32'h25C11200`, `scripts/board_rx.py freq-word`), which the BER campaign's recordings share (carrier 151,000 Hz); another recording needs its own build until a control path exists.
