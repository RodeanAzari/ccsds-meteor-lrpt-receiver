# board_rx_eth

- **Commit:** `013d5e35b508ab358698d313480ed8a11dbc5c99 (with uncommitted changes)`
- **Command:** `PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/board_rx.py check data/synthetic/synthetic_seed2026_1024000SPS.cu8 data/synthetic/synthetic_seed2026_truth.cadu --iface 169.254.235.36 --speed 1,1,1,1,1,5,5,5,5,5,5.6,5.6,5.6,5.6,5.6 --bit build/board/arty_rx_eth_top.bit`
- **Tools:** Python 3.14.4, NumPy 2.5.4, SciPy 1.18.1
- **Input:** synthetic, synthetic_seed2026_1024000SPS.cu8 (make synthetic-iq, or a recording in data/MANIFEST.md) streamed by gs-replay over 100 Mb/s Ethernet to arty_rx_eth_top on the Arty A7-100T, one replay per row, each from a freshly programmed board; truth synthetic_seed2026_truth.cadu. No real recording.

The first decode on hardware: the whole receiver on the Arty A7-100T (`arty_rx_eth_top`, built with this recording's NCO word) turned a recording replayed over Ethernet into CADUs sent back over Ethernet. The recording is `make synthetic-iq`'s (300 CADUs of OQPSK at 8 dB Eb/N0, 151 kHz with a 30 Hz/s ramp, 34 s; SatDump 1.2.2 decoded 258 of its 300 frames, `docs/log.md` 2026-11-03). In all 15 replays, each from a freshly programmed board, at 1×, 5× and 5.6× real time (5.69× is the 100 Mb/s line rate), the board sent 298 CADU packets with consecutive pkt_seq and every one matched a sent frame on all 892 data bytes, with no codeword uncorrectable. The first frame is lost to the loops' pull-in and the last is cut by the end of the recording, as in simulation. Nothing was lost on the board at any speed: no eth_rx overflow, no MAC restart, no frame-sync slip or dropped peak; every datagram reached the ingest (ingest_ok = 68,266 = all sent). The Reed-Solomon corrections are the host's doing: the 7 replays without datagram reordering (ingest_gap 0) needed no correction at all, and the others needed 9 to 84 symbols, rising with the reorder count (ingest_gap 3 to 21). Each reordered pair swaps 512 samples (0.5 ms) of signal; RS repaired every such burst here. The very first replay of the session (not in this table, its row overwritten when the campaign tool moved to one row per replay) had 12 reorder gaps and one frame of 298 wrong with 4 codewords uncorrectable, so heavy reordering can cost frames: a reordering buffer in the ingest stays the fix if real replays need it. The input is synthetic: this proves the receiver on hardware, not on a real Meteor signal.
