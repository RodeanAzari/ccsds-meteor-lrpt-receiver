# synth_rx_chain

- **Commit:** `ab2a53cad55fcbec1bad068759da1b7e92402da2 (with uncommitted changes)`
- **Command:** `make synth-ooc TOP=rx_chain`
- **Tools:** Python 3.14.4, NumPy 2.5.4, SciPy 1.18.1, Vivado 2025.2
- **Input:** synthetic, no input data: out-of-context place and route of rx_chain on xc7a100tcsg324-1. No real recording.

The receiver chain after the DDC (`rtl/top/rx_chain.sv`: sync_top → fec_soft_quant → fec_framesync → fec_viterbi → fec_frame_extract → fec_cadu with the fpga-rs-core decoder) closes timing out of context at 100 MHz on the xc7a100t-1 after place and route, with no failing endpoints (WNS +0.629 ns, WHS +0.073 ns). It uses 11,247 LUTs (17.7% of the device's 63,400), 8,348 flip-flops, 23 DSP48E1s (9.6% of 240), 5 RAMB36 (the frame sync's 32k-symbol ring and the Viterbi's survivor memory) and 4 RAMB18 (the Costas sine table and three in the RS decoder). Phase E adds 4,497 LUTs over the chain through the Viterbi (6,750 before): the RS decoder 4,199 (its own build at CCSDS parameters: 4,142, fpga-rs-core `docs/reports/ooc/`), fec_cadu's own logic 268 (192 of them LUTRAM: the 1 KB CADU buffer is distributed RAM, not block RAM), and fec_frame_extract, flattened into the top's own ~26 LUTs. The blocks before the Viterbi move by fewer than 130 LUTs against the previous build, so per-instance rows remain comparable only roughly; the slack in every row is the chain's. The worst path is new: from fec_cadu's buffer read address into the RS syndrome accumulators, which sets WNS 0.2 ns lower than before (+0.827 ns). The synthesis warnings are the blocks' own (the frame sync's 4-deep descriptor FIFO kept in registers; one trimmed bit in sync_timing); the chain and the Phase E blocks add none, and there are no critical warnings. Out-of-context results exclude I/O and clocking; the board top adds the DDC and Ethernet.
